The idea behind Surf80

More life in your years.

The goal isn’t simply to live longer. It’s to stay healthy, strong, and capable enough to keep doing what you love for decades to come—to surf at 80, explore the world at 90, or take on a new challenge at 100.

Private by design

Your health data should stay yours.

Health information is deeply personal. Surf80 is built around a simple principle: use your data to help you, not to build a business around your data.

✓ On-device processing Your health analysis stays on your iPhone.
✓ No account required Start without creating another health profile online.
✓ No health-data backend Your personal health data is not stored on Surf80 servers.
✓ Never sold Your health data is never sold to advertisers or data brokers.
